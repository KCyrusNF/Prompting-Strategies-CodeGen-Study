def average_of_list(nums):
    return sum(nums) / len(nums)
✅ Explanation:

sum(nums) adds up all elements in the list.

len(nums) gives the total number of elements.

Dividing the total sum by the count gives the arithmetic mean (average).

Example:
print(average_of_list([2, 4, 6, 8]))  # Output: 5.0
